TUNNELRAT by Tront

TUNNELRAT is a free six-degrees-of-freedom mine crawler in the spirit of Descent. Fly a ship through procedurally generated tunnels, roll, pitch and yaw with full freedom, and clear the mine. Every run generates a new tunnel network, so no two descents are the same.

It is a playable vertical slice in Three.js, one HTML file with no install, with a headless test harness that flies the ship through the generator on every change. Made by Tront (Trent Sterling).
